## **How Ductless Units Work Their Magic**

Ductless units have an indoor unit (coil/bower pack) and an outdoor unit (the heat pump).

The outdoor unit is a heat pump. On a warm day, it removes heat and humidity extracted from your home to produce cool air. On a cool day, it extracts heat from the outdoors and transfers it into your home.

The indoor unit is small and rectangular, resembling an ultramodern light fixture. It mounts to the wall or ceiling and disperses air into the room through vents without needing ductwork.

## **Why They’re Wonderful**

Remodeling projects are fun, but what happens when you are trying to remodel a room without ductwork—such as your attic, basement, or garage? A [ductless mini split](/residential/ductless-systems/) provides you with a flexible solution.

Older century homes without ductwork can also take advantage of these systems. Other benefits include:

***Room-Specific Climate Control (Zoning)***

Because mini splits don’t use [air ducts](/residential/air-ducts/), you may need to install multiple units to provide comfort throughout your entire home. In many ways, this can be beneficial. It allows you to have individual control over temperature settings on different floors or rooms in your home. You can also cut down your energy use by only heating or cooling rooms as they’re being used.

***Enhanced Air Quality***

Ductless units are essential for families with allergies or asthma. They contain air filters that catch harmful molecules like dust, dander, and pollen. On top of that, they remove humidity from the air and provide ventilation, making it harder for mildew and mold to grow.

***Low Noise Pollution***

Standard heating and cooling equipment can emit a noise as it cycles. You may find this distracting when you’re trying to focus on work or fall asleep. In stark contrast, ductless blowers can be whisper-silent. That’s one more way they make your home a peaceful and relaxing place.

***High Efficiency***

These all-in-one heating and cooling units will reduce your energy usage drastically. Homeowners in areas where the temperature dips below freezing may consider a hyper heating system that allows heating well below 0° F. But even then, it’s possible to realize massive savings.
