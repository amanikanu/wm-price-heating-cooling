### Furnaces: Warmth on Demand

Furnaces are a popular choice for home heating in areas like Youngstown, OH. They generate heat through the combustion of natural gas, propane, or oil. The heated air is then distributed throughout your home via a network of ductwork and vents.

Modern furnaces often feature high-efficiency ratings, variable-speed blowers for consistent air delivery, and multiple stages. These features contribute to both comfort and energy savings.

**Furnace** **Lifespan:** With proper care and maintenance, a typical furnace can typically last up to 20 years. However, neglecting regular service can significantly shorten its lifespan and reduce efficiency.  
  
Factors like the quality of the original installation, repair cost, and, most importantly, annual maintenance visits play a crucial role in how long your furnace will last.

**Pro Furnace Maintenance Tip:** Schedule annual furnace maintenance once a year from a qualified HVAC company such as Wm. Price Heating and Cooling.  
  
Maintenance appointments typically include inspecting the and/or cleaning the burners, cleaning or replacing the air or fuel filters, checking the ignition system, and testing for carbon monoxide leaks along with cleaning components and condensate drains.  
  
A well-maintained furnace not only lasts longer but also operates more efficiently, saving you money on your energy bills. An important tip is to change your furnace filter regularly according to the manufacturer’s recommendation. A dirty filter restricts airflow, making your furnace work harder and shortening its lifespan.

### Boilers: Consistent and Cozy Heat

Boilers provide heat by warming water, which is then circulated through pipes to radiators or heat emitters throughout your Ohio home. Radiant heat offers a consistent and comfortable warmth, often preferred by those who dislike the forced air of a furnace. A benefit with boiler systems is that they can easily be zoned.

Modern boilers can be highly efficient and may include features like sealed combustion and electronic controls for precise temperature management.

**Boiler** **Lifespan:** Boilers are known for their durability and often last longer than furnaces. With proper maintenance, you can expect a boiler to last anywhere from 20 to 30 years, or even longer. Just like furnaces, regular maintenance is essential for maximizing a boiler’s lifespan.

**Pro Boiler** **Maintenance Tip:** Annual boiler inspections are crucial. Our licensed HVAC technician in Mahoning Valley will check the water pressure, inspect the heat exchanger, test the safety valves, carbon monoxide testing, and ensure the system is operating safely and efficiently.

Regularly flushing the boiler to remove sediment buildup can also significantly extend its lifespan. Proper water treatment is also essential to prevent corrosion and scaling, which can damage the boiler and reduce its efficiency.

### Heat Pumps Systems: Year-Round Comfort

Air-source heat pump systems offer a versatile solution for year-round comfort in Youngstown, OH. Unlike other heating systems, heat pump systems do not use fuels like natural gas, propane, or oil. Instead, they extract heat from the air outside.

In the winter, they extract heat from the outside air (yes, even cold air has heat) and transfer it inside your home. In the summer, they reverse the process and operate exactly like an air conditioner extracting heat from the inside and transferring it outside.

Modern heat pumps often feature variable-speed compressors and advanced controls for enhanced efficiency and comfort. While some consumers may think heat pumps are not effective in the cold, modern air-source heat pumps are designed to operate efficiently even in lowest of temperatures, making them a viable option for our state’s climate.

**Heat Pump** **Lifespan:** Air-source heat pumps typically last between 15 to 18 years. Proper maintenance can help maximize their longevity and ensure optimal performance.

**Pro Heat Pump** **Maintenance Tip:** Schedule annual heat pump maintenance with a qualified HVAC company. This service typically includes regularly cleaning the outdoor unit cleaning the coils, checking the refrigerant levels, inspecting the electrical components, and ensuring proper airflow. One task that Ohio homeowners can perform themselves is to regularly replace the air filter.

Don’t neglect these tasks, as they can significantly impact the efficiency and lifespan of your heat pump system.
