A Heating or Cooling system is a major investment for any [Girard](/city/girard-oh/) homeowner. Although it’s not something that will need to be done frequently, most people will face the decision of needing to purchase a new HVAC system sometime during home ownership.

Our team at [Wm Price Heating Co](/) wants you to understand how your furnace and air conditioning system works and what signs you can watch for to indicate it’s time for a replacement HVAC system.

## **Time Has Passed**

Perhaps one of the biggest factors in deciding it’s time to replace your system is its age. The average life span of a system is 15 to 20 years, but this can vary greatly depending on how well you have maintained your system. However, if your system is more than a decade old, it may be time to start planning for a [furnace](/residential/furnaces/) replacement.

## **Repairs Increase in Number, Cost, and Frequency**

Another sign that it may be time to replace your current system for a new and more efficient system is its repair record. As your system ages, you’ll find yourself calling in your Ohio heating, ventilation, and [air conditioning](/residential/air-conditioning/) (HVAC) professionals more frequently. In addition, you’ll probably see the costs of repairs start increasing as well.

## **Utility Usage Rises**

Keep an eye on your regular utility bills. If the unit cost of energy doesn’t rise but your monthly bills do, that may be another sign it’s time to replace your system. Older equipment tends to work less efficiently over time, which means it will require more energy to produce the same amount of heat or cooling.

One advantage of upgrading to a new system is that new equipment is always more efficient than older equipment and ultimately better at making your home more comfortable. As a result, you will start to recoup some of your investment because of lower monthly utility bills.

## **Excessive Humidity, Dust, or Dirt**

Although many factors can play a role in [indoor air quality](/residential/indoor-air-quality/), your system can definitely make a difference. If routine [maintenance](/assurance-and-security-plans/) like changing your air filters and regular tune-ups do not solve humidity, dust, or other air quality issues, it may be a sign you need to replace your system.

## **Strange Noises or Odors**

Be sure to be attentive to the daily behavior of your furnace. Often, buzzing, vibrations, clanking, or other noises may warrant a service call. If your professional technician finds your equipment has excessive wear or structural damage, it’s a wise decision to invest in a new system.

## **Change in Comfort Level**

When your system is operating and your home doesn’t feel as comfortable as it has in the past, you may want to explore the causes. Older systems that are working inefficiently can cause different rooms in your home to vary greatly in temperature, leading to uncomfortable cold and hot spots. If you upgrade your system to a multi stage or variable capacity high efficiency model, you may be able to boost the comfort level of you and your family as well as lowering your utility bills.

## **Physical Appearance**

Finally, you can take a quick inventory of your system’s physical appearance. If you see signs of rusting, cracks, or other structural weaknesses, it may be time to evaluate your options to upgrade your system.
