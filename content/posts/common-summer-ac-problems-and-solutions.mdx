### **AC Is Not Cooling**

If your air conditioner is simply blowing warm air, you may want to check simple things like your filter. Dirty filters are easy to change and can prevent your system from functioning properly. They can also decrease your system’s efficiency.

### **AC Has No Air Movement**

When you place your hand on a room’s register and feel no air blowing, even though your air conditioning unit is turned on, it may be a problem with the blower motor or possibly frozen indoor coil.  
  
Check your thermostat settings and see if it seems to be working properly (and that no one else in the house changed the settings) to rule it out as the source of your problem. Next, check to see if a breaker tripped—it’s easy to fix your tripped breaker on your own, just push the switch back to the on position.

### **Ice Accumulation on AC**

Ice in Ohio during the summer? It may sound weird, but it does happen. If you notice ice or a frosty layer accumulating around your indoor coil, refrigerant lines, or air conditioning unit, this is not normal and should be diagnosis by a professional.

If you notice ice accumulation around your system, the system should be turned off to allow time for it to defrost prior to a service technician arriving. A common cause for a “frozen” system is poor air flow. Dirty air filters, closed dampers, or blocked vents or ducts can restrict airflow and cause ice to form on the unit.

Another sign of an issue with your air conditioning is if you notice water leaking. This could be from a clogged drain or possibly a frozen indoor coil. If you notice any of these issues, call our office for one of our professionally trained technicians to come and evaluate the system.
