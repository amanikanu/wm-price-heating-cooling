## **Common HVAC Noises and What They Mean**

What is your HVAC system trying to tell you? Here is a guide to some typical sounds and their likely causes:

### **My AC is Screaming or Has High-Pitched Whining**

If your air conditioner sounds like the victim in a horror film, don’t ignore it. Screaming or shrieking can signal:

- A refrigerant leak
- Excess pressure inside the compressor

**What to do:** Turn the system off immediately and call a licensed HVAC technician. Operating under these conditions could damage your unit or create safety issues.

### **Why Is My AC Squealing or Screeching?**

These high-pitched sounds are often described as metal scraping against metal or a squeaky belt. Possible causes include:

- A worn-out fan belt
- Motor bearings that need lubrication
- A failing blower motor

**Pro HVAC tip:** Replacing belts and lubricating internal components is not a DIY job. Call the experts at Wm. Price Heating and Cooling for fast, reliable service.

### **What Should I Do if My AC Is Rattling?**

Rattling can have multiple sources, depending on where you hear it:

- At the register – Loose grille or screws
- Inside ducts – Debris, such as a nail, small toy, or insulation fragments
- From the outdoor unit – Debris stuck in the fan, loose fan blades, or failing motor parts

**Recommendation:** If it’s inside your ducts or outdoor unit, it’s time to call in a professional. You may also benefit from a thorough duct cleaning.

### **My Air Conditioner Is Banging or Clanking**

These heavier, metallic noises suggest a more serious issue:

- Loose or broken parts rattling around inside
- Unbalanced furnace blower
- Loose or damaged compressor

**Act quickly:** These sounds typically indicate components that are deteriorating and may cause system-wide damage if ignored.

### **There Is a Scraping Noise Coming From My AC**

Metal-on-metal scraping usually signals:

- Loose or broken blower wheel
- Damaged or misaligned fan blades
- Foreign objects inside the system

**Take action now:** Continuing to run your system could cause further damage to your motor or fan assembly.

### **Loud Humming, Clicking, or Buzzing Coming From My AC**

While some low humming is normal, an increase in volume or frequency could mean:

- Loose electrical wiring
- Faulty capacitor
- Failing thermostat

**Call a pro:** Electrical problems can worsen quickly and may pose safety risks if left unchecked.

### **Why Is My Air Conditioner Hissing**

A hissing HVAC system is never a good sign. Causes can include:

- Refrigerant leaks in your air conditioner or heat pump
- Leaky ductwork (especially if the sound is coming from walls or attic)
- Compressor issues

**Solution:** Only a certified HVAC technician can safely diagnose and repair refrigerant or duct leaks.
