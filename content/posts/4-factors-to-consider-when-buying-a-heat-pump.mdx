## **How to Choose a Heat Pump for Your Mahoning Valley Home**

Not all [heat pumps](/residential/heat-pumps/) are the same, and not every system is right for every home. Below are four key questions to ask yourself when shopping for the right heat pump:

### **1. How Much Are Heat Pumps?**

Heat pumps offer excellent energy efficiency. Cold climate heat pumps are some of the most efficient HVAC options available today. These systems use minimal electricity to transfer heat, which can dramatically reduce your utility bills over time.

However, it’s important to understand that many of the highest-efficiency models come with a higher initial investment compared to traditional [furnaces](/residential/furnaces/) or air conditioners. While the upfront investment may be slightly more, the long-term savings often help to offset the cost.

A general industry guideline: the more you invest up front, the more comfort you can enjoy. Still, you’ll want to weigh your budget, energy goals, and how long you plan to stay in your home before making a final decision.

### **2. What Type of Heat Pump Do I Want?**

There are several types of heat pumps, and each one works a bit differently:

- **Air-source heat pumps** are the most common. They transfer heat between your home and the outside air—providing heating in the winter and cooling in the summer. Thanks to recent technological achievements, modern air-source heat pumps perform well in a wide range of climates. According to the U.S. Department of Energy, “an air-source heat pump can deliver one-and-a-half to three times more heat energy to a home than the electrical energy it consumes.”

- **[Ductless mini splits](/residential/ductless-systems/)** operate much like air-source pumps, but they don’t require ductwork. This makes them ideal for older homes, new additions, homes with boilers, or rooms that are difficult to heat and cool.

Each type has pros and cons. The right one for your Mahoning Valley home will depend on factors such as the type of home, energy goals, and budget.

### **3. Will I Need a Dual-Fuel System?**

While many modern heat pumps work efficiently in colder weather, some models can struggle when outdoor temperatures dip well below freezing. If you live in an area where winters are consistently frigid, you may want to consider a [dual-fuel system](/products/carrier/).

A dual-fuel setup combines a heat pump with a backup heating source such as a gas furnace or oil furnace. When outdoor temperatures drop below the “balance” point where the heat pump can provide enough heat, the secondary system automatically takes over to keep your home comfortable without compromising energy efficiency.

Our Wm. Price Heating and Cooling HVAC professionals can assess your home’s layout and comfort needs to help you determine whether a dual-fuel system makes sense for your situation.

### **4. What Type of Thermostat Should I Buy?**

Your [thermostat](/products/accessories/) plays a crucial role in managing your heat pump’s performance and energy usage. Fortunately, there are several types of thermostats to choose from, depending on your comfort preferences and lifestyle:

- **Standard Thermostats** offer manual controls and a simple, easy-to-use interface. You can manually set your preferred temperature and toggle between Heat, Cool, and Fan modes.

- **Wi-Fi or Smart Thermostats** go one step further, offering remote control from your smartphone or tablet. Some models also provide energy usage reports and learning capabilities that adapt to your habits.

- **Advanced Communicating Thermostats** allows your system to be fully integrated and operate at peak efficiency. The thermostat communicates with the system adjusting airflow and staging. It also allows advanced diagnostics minimizing down time during a failure. Many advanced communicating thermostats offer Wi-Fi connectivity along with an easy-to-use app to control and monitor your system remotely for virtually anywhere.
