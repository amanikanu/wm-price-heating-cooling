## **How Do  Zoning Systems Work?**

[Zoning](/residential/zone-control-systems/) is all about dampers. When we install a system, we place automated dampers in your existing ductwork that open or close depending on the cooling needed in each zone.

The thermostats in each zone are connected to a centralized control panel, which directs dampers to open or close and signals your air conditioning system to turn on.

Zoning allows you to set the temperature for the living room at one level, keep the bedrooms at another setting, and even turn off the conditioned air in an unused guest room. It’s truly personalized comfort on a zone-by-zone basis, all while reducing energy consumption.

## **How Would HVAC Zoning Benefit Me?**

A zoning system offers significant benefits for Girard, OH homeowners.

**Customized Comfort:** No more fighting over the thermostat! Family members can enjoy their preferred temperature in their own space without impacting the rest of the house. Single homeowners can customize comfort by cooling the zones they consistently use and maintaining warmer temperatures in those they don’t.

**Comfort for Pets**: Cooling selected areas when leaving the home can benefit households of all sizes with pets.

The U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) recommends keeping your house warmer than normal when you are away from home in the summer. The DOE states you can save 10 percent on energy bills by dialing your thermostat back by 7-10° Fahrenheit for eight hours daily.

But what about your pets? While you save 10 percent on cooling costs, your furry friends may be miserable or even overheat in warmer temperatures at home. Zoning can allow you to choose warmer temperatures in areas off-limits to your pets, while you cool the places where they sleep, play, and eat.

**Hot and Cold Spots:**  Is your upstairs a sauna in the summer while the basement is a freezer? Hot and cold spots are a common issue in multi-story homes. Zoning remedies these temperature imbalances by directing conditioned air where it’s needed. If a room gets a lot of sun and heats up quickly, its thermostat can call for more cool air, creating consistent comfort throughout your home.

**Energy Efficiency:**  Why waste money cooling unoccupied rooms? By only cooling the zones you use, your AC system runs less frequently and more efficiently.  Zoning also reduces energy consumption and can significantly save on your monthly energy bills.

## **Is My Home a Good Candidate for HVAC Zoning?**

Nearly all homes benefit from the comfort and efficiency of a zoning system. Some houses are especially well-suited for this upgrade:

- **Large Homes:** The bigger the house, the more likely you will need to customize temperatures and eliminate hot and cold spots.

- **Multi-Story Homes**: Zoning is the perfect solution for balancing temperatures between the upper and lower levels, where heat naturally rises.

- **Homes with Unused Rooms**: Zoning is ideal for preventing energy waste. You simply dial back the thermostat or close off cooling to rarely used rooms.

- **Homes with Sunny Spaces**: Rooms with large, sunny windows heat up much faster than shaded rooms. Zoning allows you to compensate for this solar gain by providing extra cooling when and where it’s needed.

- **Homes with Finished Basements:** Zoning can make these lower-level spaces as comfortable as the main living areas, without impacting the temperatures in those areas.
