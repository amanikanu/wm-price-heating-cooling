## **Why Isn’t My Furnace Heating?**

Here are some of the most common reasons why furnaces stop heating effectively and guidance on what you can do to restore warmth to your home. Whether it’s a simple fix or a sign that you need professional help, being informed will help you make the right decisions for your heating system and ensure your home stays comfortable.

***1.******Lack of maintenance***

When you don’t schedule [routine maintenance](/assurance-and-security-plans/) visits, your system can lose its finesse. Dirty coils, worn/unsecured belts, and clogged filters are common mishaps. Due to these issues, you’ll have a compromised heating system.

Routine maintenance saves you money. You can reduce your energy costs by up to 30 percent with seasonal tune-ups and professional maintenance. Regular heating and cooling maintenance can also reduce costly breakdowns by as much as 95 percent.

***2.******Improperly sized furnace***

When a furnace is sized incorrectly for your Ohio home, it will usually produce poor heating. Undersized units lack the capacity to heat your entire living area.

Similarly, larger units short-cycle, leading to inadequate [heating](/residential/furnaces/). One of our experts can perform heat load calculations to determine whether your system is the correct size.

***3.******Thermostat problems***

Try changing your [thermostat](/products/carrier/) settings. If your furnace fan is set to *Auto*, change it to the *On* setting, helping to eliminate stale air creating a more balanced and even temperature throughout the home.

Your thermostat might also have heat sensor issues or faulty wiring, which causes it to produce the wrong temperature reading. A professional can diagnose this issue.

***4.******Wrong-sized filter***

Good airflow is key to dispersing hot air throughout your home. If your filter is too thick for your furnace, it creates excessive resistance, leading to compromised airflow. You can have your technician perform a static resistance test to see if it’s the right size.

***5.******HVAC zoning***

Some homes have varied heating needs for different rooms. Fortunately, with a [zone control system](/residential/zone-control-systems/), you can adjust the temperature settings in multiple areas. If you live in a home with multiple stories, large windows, or a furnished basement, zone controls may be the best solution.

***6.******Duct problems***

Leaky or blocked ducts can lead to patchy heating. Leaks in your ducts can cause uneven heating. If you think you might have leaky ducts, have a trained technician scheduled to evaluate your duct work.

Also, take a walk around inside your home to see if any registers or intake vents are being blocked by furniture or carpeting.

***7.******Poor insulation***

If your ducts, windows, attic, or walls have poor or nonexistent insulation, you might have a problem. Heat is escaping, which can cause inconsistent heating. However, you can fix this relatively quickly.

Your local insulation professional can perform a thermal analysis to figure out which areas need insulation. A professional can install insulation within hours, and compared to other home improvements, it is relatively affordable.
