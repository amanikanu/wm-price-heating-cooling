Breakdowns are not inevitable. Here are three ways to insulate your home from an air conditioning malfunction during a heat wave:

You will welcome the restored efficiency when your [thermostat](/products/accessories/) is able to be set for your comfort knowing your system is operating properly and efferently.   As summer rolls on and the heat intensifies, scheduling a repair technician at your preferred time can be challenging.

- **Upgrade to High-Efficiency AC**If your air conditioner is over 12-15 years old, needs frequent repairs, or has significant problems, replacing it with a high-efficiency unit will improve reliability, save [money](/site-map/) on repairs and energy bills, improving cooling and [humidity](/products/carrier/)-reducing performance.  
    
  A high-efficiency air conditioner uses less energy than your outdated system and reduces your impact on the environment. A two stage scroll compressor or  variable capacity inverter-driven compressor technology adjusts its speed to deliver the precise cooling and humidity control needed.  
    
  Once the system starts, the system will ramp up and down, to match cooling capacity, allowing the system to reach the desired temperature on your thermostat efficiently. As the system ramps up and down, energy consumption is reduced and the system can operate for a longer interval helping to reduce hot and cold spots in a home maintaining a precise temperature and humidity level.  
    
  A conventional compressor turns on, runs at capacity until it reaches the desired temperature, and then turns off. When the indoor temperature falls below the set point, the compressor turns on again. Constant stopping and starting expends energy consumption and shortens component lifespan through wear and tear as well creates uneven temperatures throughout a home.  
    
  High-efficiency air conditioners also are quiet and better at reducing humidity.

- **AC Air Filter**Replacing your air filter is an easy maintenance task that yields significant results. Your air conditioner runs more frequently during a heat wave. If it operates with a dirty, debris-clogged air filter, it loses efficiency and might restrict airflow causing a failure of a blower motor or indoor coil to freeze. A dirty filter could lead to a breakdown and costly repair.  
    
  Check your filter at least monthly during a prolonged heat wave. Refer to your professionals for the filters your system should use.
