We all know the difference between the heating and cooling settings on a [thermostat](/products/accessories/).

But what’s the difference between the On or Auto switch? How does this setting impact the [heating](/residential/furnaces/), [ventilation](/residential/indoor-air-quality/), and [air conditioning](/residential/air-conditioning/) (HVAC) system in your [Girard](/city/girard-oh/) home?

Switching the setting to On will leave your HVAC fan continuously running, even after your system reaches a targeted temperature. The Auto setting, on the other hand, means the fan will only run while your system is actively heating or cooling the air inside your [Ohio](/service-area/) home or business.

Your preferred setting can depend on several factors, from cost savings to improving your [indoor air quality](/residential/indoor-air-quality/) (IAQ). Here is some basic information from our team at [Wm Price Heating Co](/) to get you to think about a strategy that works best for you and your loved ones.

## **Why Set My Thermostat to Auto?**

The best part of leaving your thermostat to Auto is you never have to think about it again. The fan turns on when the furnace or air conditioner starts running and goes off when your home reaches a set temperature. It’s a simple, no-fuss, energy-efficient approach to your heating and cooling needs.

According to ENERGY STAR®, heating and cooling your home accounts for nearly half of your annual energy bill. Finding ways to reduce these costs can turn into a real money saver.

## **Why Set My Thermostat to On?**

More air circulation means cleaner air. Since the air in your home continually runs through a filter, this setting may be better for family members who suffer from allergies or asthma. You may also want to leave the fan setting to On if you live in an industrial setting or someone smokes in the house.

Leaving the fan setting to On also helps distribute the air more evenly, which helps to eliminate “hot” and “cold” spots in your [Girard](/city/girard-oh/) home. Another benefit is your fan no longer starts and stops throughout the day, which may help to extend its service life.

## **Which Thermostat Setting Works Best?**

Our team at [Wm Price Heating Co](/) recommends that your thermostat be set to your own individual and your family comfort requirements.

While leaving the [thermostat](/products/accessories/) in the On setting does add many benefits, including a better job of circulating the air, it also means you will use more energy and may have to replace HVAC filters more often. Although it might be a little extra on your utility bills and you also need to replace filters more frequently, there are many health benefits with constantly filtering your indoor air.

Occasionally cooler winter months, it might feel like cold air is coming through the vents. It’s not cold air; it’s just room temperature air. It only seems colder compared to your body temperature because it is not being heated.

Likewise, the Auto setting can have several advantages and a few disadvantages. You can always test the difference to see which one you prefer.

Some clients prefer turning the thermostat setting to On during the day and back to Auto before you go to bed. Another way clients can enhance the comfort of their [Girard](/city/girard-oh/) home is by turning the thermostat to On in the cooling season and to Auto in the heating season.
