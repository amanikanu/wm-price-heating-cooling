One of the most common mistakes in HVAC system replacement is incorrect system sizing. An oversized or undersized system can lead to inefficiencies, performance issues, and an uncomfortable house. ### **Quality Ductwork**

The ductwork is a critical component of how well your HVAC system will function and is responsible for distributing conditioned air throughout your home. Your HVAC system’s air conditioning system must be properly installed to ensure efficient and reliable operation. Improper installation practices can cause the system to freeze, reduced capacity on the hottest days, and lead to compressor and thermostatic expansion valve failure. Wm.
