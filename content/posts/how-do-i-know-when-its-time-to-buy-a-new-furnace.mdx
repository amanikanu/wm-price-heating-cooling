## **Signs You Need to Replace Your Furnace**

Here are several key signs that indicate it’s time for a furnace replacement:

- **Your furnace is approaching or at the end of its life expectancy**If you’ve kept up with routine professional maintenance over the years, your furnace could last its full service life of 15 to 20 years. Consider the age of your furnace, even if it turns on and off, it could be operating at reduced efficiency and affecting your comfort and energy usage. Older systems are less energy-efficient and more prone to breakdowns.  
    
  [Maintenance](/assurance-and-security-plans/) plays a vital role in the health of your HVAC system. Always be sure to schedule annual maintenance for your heating and cooling [equipment](/products/).
- **The system is no longer operating efficiently**Are you worried about setting your [thermostat](/products/accessories/) where you are comfortable because of energy bills? An inefficient furnace not only limits your ability to keep comfortable but can even continue to raise your energy bills with keeping the thermostat at a lower setting.  
    
  If your system is inefficient, you could benefit by upgrading to a newer, more energy-efficient model. A more efficient furnace will use less energy to operate and provide an adequate amount of heat all throughout your home, so you’ll be left feeling cozier than you ever have.  
    
  The newer furnaces that are available today are far more efficient than older models. Opt for an ENERGY STAR® qualified furnace, which can be up to 15 percent more efficient than traditional furnaces, according to ENERGY STAR.
- **Your heating bills are more expensive than they once were**Your furnace could be to culprit for your increasing energy expenses. As systems age, they lose their efficiency and have to work harder to maintain the desired temperature, leading to higher energy consumption and increased costs. Upgrading to a newer model can provide a more energy-efficient system that improves comfort and lowers your monthly heating costs.
- **You’ve already spent a good amount of money on several repairs**If you find yourself calling for repairs frequently, it could be more cost-effective to invest in a new system rather than continually fixing an outdated one. Frequent repairs can add up quickly and indicate that your system is nearing the end of its useful life. Even if repairs are possible, a system at the end of its life will still need to be replaced.
- **Uneven Heating or Cooling**If some rooms in your home are too hot or too cold while others are comfortable, your HVAC system may not be distributing air properly. This could be due to issues with the ductwork, the system itself, or an undersized or oversized unit. Addressing this problem can improve overall comfort and efficiency.

- **Replacement considerations**  
  The older your furnace is, the more likely it is to struggle with providing the appropriate amount comfort, unable to support [indoor air quality](/residential/indoor-air-quality/) (IAQ) products or smart thermostats. If you suffer from allergies to asthma, the lack of ideal air quality could leave you feeling terrible in your home. You may notice symptoms like wheezing, sneezing, or coughing. A newer furnace will be able to provide the latest in indoor air quality such as an air purification system or a whole home [humidifier](/products/carrier/). Pairing your system with a smart thermostat will allow maximum control, energy savings, and can notify you if you need to address any issues such as maintenance or replacing filters, keeping your home cozy and healthy year round.
